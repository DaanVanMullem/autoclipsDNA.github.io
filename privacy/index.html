AutoClip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoClip ("the Service") handles information when you use it to create short videos and publish them to social accounts you connect.

Information we process

  • Connected accounts: when you authorise a platform (e.g. TikTok, YouTube), we store the access/refresh tokens the platform issues so the Service can publish on your behalf. We do not receive your password.
  • Basic profile: your account username/display name, used to show which account you are posting to.
  • Content you provide: the source videos and the clips generated from them, plus the titles, descriptions and hashtags produced for each post.

How we use it

Solely to operate the Service: to generate clips and to upload/publish them to the accounts you explicitly authorise. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Content and metadata are sent only to the platforms you choose to publish to, through their official APIs, to complete the posts you request.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or delete the data. Disconnecting an account revokes the Service's ability to post and removes the stored tokens.

Your choices

You can disconnect any account at any time, and you may revoke access from the platform's own security settings. You can request deletion of stored data using the contact below.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.

Contact: the operator of this service (set a contact email to display it).
